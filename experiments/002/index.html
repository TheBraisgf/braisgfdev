#002 · SOFTWARE · 2026

Build a better
digital reader.

What would a reading engine look like if it were designed by someone who cares obsessively about books, comics and the way interfaces get out of the reader's way?

HYPOTHESISMost readers optimize for features. A great reader should optimize for immersion.
OBJECTIVEPrototype a reading engine from scratch and test its assumptions.
TOOLSWeb platform · UX · TypeScript · typography · EPUB/CBZ research.
STATUSIDEA

The interesting part

This experiment deliberately sits at the intersection of software, design and geek culture. It could become a tool, a public prototype, a video series, or all three.

The first milestone is not “build a competitor to every reader”. It is finding one reading problem that is genuinely worth solving.

Imagine. Code. Create. Share. Repeat.
