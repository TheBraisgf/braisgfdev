#003 · LEARNING · 2026

Learn by building
the thing.

What if the fastest way to learn a skill is to make something that is slightly beyond what you can currently do?

HYPOTHESISReal constraints create better learning than endless passive study.
OBJECTIVEChoose one unfamiliar skill and ship a small finished artifact.
RULENo “I’ll start when I’m good enough”. The project starts first.
STATUSPLANNED

The experiment

The exact skill is intentionally not locked yet. The point is to test the learning method itself: define a tiny outcome, identify the minimum knowledge required, build immediately, document every mistake and finish something that can actually be shown.

This could be drawing, electronics, animation, 3D modelling, writing or another rabbit hole. The result matters less than proving that curiosity can be turned into a curriculum.

What counts as success?

A finished artifact, a visible before-and-after in the skill, and a written account of what actually accelerated learning — including the parts that didn't work.
