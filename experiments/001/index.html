#001 · META · 2026

Make the lab
worth visiting.

Can a personal website become a place people actually want to return to — instead of a page that exists because every developer is supposed to have one?

HYPOTHESISA personal lab feels more interesting when it documents attempts, not just achievements.
OBJECTIVECreate a repeatable format for ideas, builds, failures and results.
CONSTRAINTIt must remain fast, responsive and cheap to host.
STATUSRUNNING

Why this experiment exists

I have too many interests to build a traditional portfolio around one profession. Programming is only one part of what I make. The experiment format gives every interest the same container without pretending they are the same hobby.

The site itself is the first test subject. If this becomes useful, the next experiments get better dossiers: progress logs, media, source files, decisions, failures and results.

What counts as success?

Not traffic for its own sake. Success means someone discovers an experiment, understands why it exists, gets something useful or entertaining from it, and wants to see what happens next.
